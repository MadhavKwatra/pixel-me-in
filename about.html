About Pixel Me In

The idea

In the Phineas and Ferb episode "Gaming the System" (season 2, 2009), the brothers build a video game and zap themselves into it with a digitizer. This game asks: what if you could do that with your own face, right in your browser?

It is a fan-made tribute. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Disney, and it uses none of the show's characters, names or art.

Who made it

Madhav Kwatra – full-stack developer. I built this to learn game development and on-device machine learning, and because it sounded fun.

How it is built

  • Phaser 4 runs the game. Menus and pages are plain HTML.
  • MediaPipe finds your face and cuts out your head – in your browser.
  • A small pixel filter (written for this game) picks colours from your photo and keeps your eyes and mouth readable.
  • All art, music and sound effects are made in code. There are no image or sound files.
  • It installs as an app and works offline.

Open source

All the code is public and MIT licensed: read it, check that your photo stays on your device, copy it, or make it better. Ideas, bug reports and new hats are welcome.
