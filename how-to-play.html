How to play

You run by yourself. Jump over things on the ground, slide under things in the air, and grab the gears. Every 500 m you are zapped into a new world.

Controls

Touch screen

Jump
Tap the right half of the screen. Tap again in the air to double jump.
Slide
Hold the left half. In the air, this drops you down fast.
Pause
The pause button in the top right corner.

Turn your phone sideways to play.

Keyboard

Jump
Space, ↑ or W (or click). Press again in the air to double jump. Let go early for a small hop.
Slide
Hold ↓ or S. In the air, this drops you down fast.
Pause
Esc or P.

Gamepad

Jump
A or d-pad up.
Slide
B or d-pad down.
Pause
Start.

Power-ups

The first one shows up at about 120 m, then at least one every 250 m.

Shield bubble
Survive one crash.
Gear magnet
Pulls nearby gears to you for 8 seconds.
Jetpack
Fly over everything for 5 seconds.

The four worlds

Backyard, rollercoaster, beach and space. Near the end of each world the robot chases you and things come faster. Survive, and you get zapped into the next one.

First run

Your first run teaches you the moves. Time slows down until you do each one: jump, slide, double jump, grab a power-up. You can replay it from How to play in the game.

Unlocks

Long runs and gears unlock new looks in the Photo Booth:

  • Crown and space helmet: run 1,500 m in one go.
  • 3D glasses: collect 150 gears in total.
  • Space suit: run 5,000 m in total.
  • Lab coat: play 10 runs.

Tips

  • Press jump a moment before you land – it still counts.
  • Hold slide in the air to drop fast and slide right away.
  • Slow mode and reduce motion are in Settings.
