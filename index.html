Pixel Me In
How to play Privacy About

Put your face in a pixel-art game.

Take a selfie. Watch it turn into pixels. Then run, jump and slide through a backyard, a rollercoaster, a beach and outer space – with a giant robot on your heels.

Works on phones, tablets and computers. No account. Your photo never leaves your device.

How to play

Your character runs by itself. Jump over things on the ground. Slide under things in the air. Grab gears. Every 500 m you are zapped into a new world. Just before each zap, the robot chases you and everything gets faster.

Controls
Action Keyboard Touch Gamepad
Jump (tap again in the air for a double jump) Space, ↑ or W, or click Tap the right half A
Slide (in the air: drop fast) ↓ or S Hold the left half B or ↓
Pause Esc or P Pause button Start

Power-ups (inventions)

  • Shield bubble – survive one crash.
  • Gear magnet – pulls nearby gears to you for 8 seconds.
  • Jetpack – fly over everything for 5 seconds.

Tips

  • Let go of jump early for a small hop.
  • Gears and long runs unlock new hats and outfits in the Photo Booth.
  • Turn on Slow mode or Reduce motion in Settings if you need them.

Privacy: your photo stays on your device

  • The photo is read by your browser. It is never uploaded. There is no server.
  • Face finding runs on your device (MediaPipe, hosted on this same site).
  • Only a small cropped face (256×256) is saved, in your browser's storage. The full photo is thrown away, along with its hidden data (like GPS location).
  • “Delete my data” in Settings removes everything.
  • A security rule (Content-Security-Policy connect-src 'self') makes the browser block any connection to other sites.

Do not just trust us – check it: open your browser's developer tools, go to the Network tab, and take a photo. Nothing leaves. The source code is public, too.

About

Pixel Me In is a fan-made, open-source game inspired by a cartoon episode where two brothers build a video game and zap themselves into it. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any studio.

Built with Phaser, TypeScript, Vite and MediaPipe. Contributions welcome.
